# SharedScience.me — Use Cases

## 1. Supercomputing Meets Cryogenics (The Original Scenario)

**Org A** operates a supercomputing facility with novel simulation capabilities for materials science. They need expertise in cryogenic preservation to solve a scaling problem.

**Org B** is a biotech lab with a breakthrough in flash-freezing biological tissue but lacks computational power for large-scale modeling.

**Flow**:
- Org B publishes: *"Verified advances in cryogenic preservation of biological tissue at scales > 1cm³"* (ZK proof — no methods revealed)
- Org A searches domain: Physics > Thermodynamics > Cryogenics
- Org A finds Org B's proof, sends anonymous inquiry
- Progressive disclosure leads to collaboration agreement
- IP terms define shared ownership of any combined breakthroughs

## 2. Pharmaceutical Drug Discovery

A pharma company has a novel compound showing promise for Alzheimer's treatment but needs a specific drug delivery mechanism they don't have.

- Publishes: *"Compound with demonstrated efficacy against amyloid-beta aggregation in vitro"*
- Searches for: *"Novel blood-brain barrier penetration mechanisms"*
- Finds a biotech startup with exactly that capability
- Neither reveals their trade secrets until a formal agreement is in place

## 3. Defense-to-Commercial Technology Transfer

A defense lab has developed advanced sensor technology under a classified program. They want to explore commercial applications but can't reveal the technology.

- Publishes: *"Sensor capability: detection of [material class] at distances > 100m with accuracy > 99.5%"*
- Commercial manufacturers searching for quality control sensors find the proof
- ITAR-compliant capability advertising — proves WHAT without revealing HOW

## 4. Cross-University Research Collaboration

Two universities working on different aspects of the same problem don't know about each other.

- University A publishes: *"Novel gene editing approach for [specific protein family]"*
- University B publishes: *"Computational model for [same protein family] folding behavior"*
- Both discover each other through domain taxonomy search
- Collaborate before publication — strengthening both papers

## 5. AI/ML Capability Matching

An AI lab has trained a model with exceptional performance on a specific task but needs domain data to validate it.

- Publishes: *"Model achieving > 95% accuracy on [task type] with < 100ms inference time"*
- Does NOT reveal architecture, training data, or weights
- A hospital system with relevant medical imaging data connects
- Data never leaves the hospital — model performance verified through federated approach

## 6. Climate Technology

A startup has developed a carbon capture method but needs industrial engineering partners to scale.

- Publishes: *"Carbon capture efficiency > 90% at atmospheric CO2 concentrations"*
- Industrial engineering firms search for climate tech capabilities
- Collaboration progresses through staged disclosure
- IP agreement defines licensing terms before methods are shared

## 7. Quantum Computing Problem Matching

A quantum computing facility wants to find commercially relevant problems that benefit from quantum advantage — without revealing their specific qubit count or error rates.

- Publishes: *"Quantum optimization capability for problems with > 1000 variables"*
- Logistics companies, financial firms, and drug designers find the proof
- Problem-capability matching without exposing competitive advantages
