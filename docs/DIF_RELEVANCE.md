## Why DIF matters for SharedScience

SharedScience researcher-credential and selective-disclosure use cases align with Claims and Credentials WG outputs and BBS+ signatures. Credential Manifest standardizes how journals or institutions issue researcher credentials. Presentation Exchange standardizes how peer reviewers prove credentials without revealing identity.

## DIF specs to adopt

- **Claims and Credentials WG**: full alignment, researcher credentials are a textbook use case
- **BBS+ Signatures**: selective disclosure of credential attributes (degree, affiliation, specialization)
- **Credential Manifest**: institutional credential issuance flow
- **Presentation Exchange**: peer-review credential checks
- **WACI-DIDComm**: researcher wallet integration
